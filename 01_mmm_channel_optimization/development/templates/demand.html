Demand trends and the next-quarter lock

Forecasting plus diagnostic analytics on national weekly demand. The point is not a prettier line — it is to see whether the category is accelerating or softening, separate trend from season and promo, and give planning a volume number with a band. {% set howto_model %}

13-week trend + week-of-year season + residual, then seasonal naive versus Ridge ARX on the level. Ensemble by inverse holdout error. Horizon uses known calendar and frozen promo / price. Grow / Hold / Cut compares the next 13 weeks to the last 13 and to last year.

Demand forecasting

Promo-aware weekly demand models for five retail channels. The forecast is built to decide trade spend — TPR, feature, and display — not just to draw a line. Seasonal naive is the baseline. Ridge ARX adds price, holiday, payday, and promo drivers. An inverse-RMSE ensemble is what we score on holdout. Allocation of the trade budget is on the next page. National trend, residual diagnostics, and the next-quarter volume lock live on Demand trends.

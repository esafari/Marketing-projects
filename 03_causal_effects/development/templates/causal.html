Separate true impact from correlation

A 12% lift is planted at a known week. In the confounded rollout, the fastest-growing geos get the treatment — the usual commercial story. Observational pre/post then overstates impact. Difference-in-differences and a randomized geo experiment are scored against the same truth so a stakeholder can see why “it went up after we spent” is not a decision.

{% set howto_model %}

Geo-week panel. True multiplicative lift on treated geos after the cut. Methods: treated-only pre/post, difference-in-differences, two-way demeaned OLS, and an elasticity-style log contrast. The same lift is also assigned at random as the experiment.

{% endset %} {% set howto_test %}

Click Run causal (20 geos, 80 weeks, 6 treated, seed 42). Pass if observational bias is clearly larger than the randomized difference-in-differences bias, and the treated line in the confounded chart was already rising before the cut.

{% endset %} {% set howto_diag %}

Observational ≈ experiment: the confound was weak (growth gaps too small). Difference-in-differences still biased in the confounded world: trends were not parallel — that is the point of the randomized column. Do not brief the observational number to finance.
